# Simulations

Each simulation is a machine wired to the PLC's input and output cards. Pick one in the
**Simulations** menu; its panel shows the machine, its buttons and switches, and the address of
every device. Drag any labelled device onto an instruction or a pin to use its address.

The machine reacts to your outputs and sets your inputs every scan. Many of them can be damaged or
misused (a door closing on a car, a tank overflowing): those events are reported in the bar under
the drawing, and the exercises check for them.

Every simulation has an example program in **File → Open example**.

The I/O of each one is in the [simulation reference](reference/simulations.md).
