Structured text functions¶

Functions callable in expressions, with their number of arguments. Conversions X_TO_Y work between BOOL, SINT, INT, DINT, LINT, REAL, LREAL, TIME, WORD, DWORD and BYTE.

Blocks called on their tag: Tag.TON(IN := …, PT := …), TOF, TP, RTO, CTU (CU, R, PV), CTD (CD, LD, PV), PID (PV, SP, CV =>). Routines and blocks: JSR(Name);, "Function"(In := x, Out => y);, "Instance"(…);.
